Dilhan Manawadu

Department of Chemistry; University of Liverpool

We are a newly established quantum computational chemistry group at the University of Liverpool. We design and implement quantum computing algorithms for chemistry and quantum machine learning.

An overview of our research aims, methods and recent highlights can be found on the Research page, and a full list of our publications on the Publications page. You can meet the team on the People page, and if you are interested in working with us, see Join Us.

Research interests: Theoretical and Computational Chemistry · Quantum Machine Learning · Tensor Network Methods (DMRG) · Quantum Computing.
