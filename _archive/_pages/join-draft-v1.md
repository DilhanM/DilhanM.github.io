Our research sits between chemistry, physics and computer science. We welcome people with a background in any of these, or a related subject. Some programming experience (e.g. Python) is helpful, but you do not need to be an expert in quantum computing already.

#### PhD students

PhD positions in the group are funded through studentships and doctoral training programmes at the University of Liverpool. Good places to start are the [Chemistry PhD](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/courses/chemistry-phd) page, the University's [Doctoral Training Programmes](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/postgraduate-research/research-opportunities/doctoral-training-programmes/), and the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in [Digital and Automated Materials Chemistry](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/doctoral-training/digital-and-automated-materials-chemistry/prospective-students/). If you are not sure which funding route fits, get in touch and we can work it out together.

#### Postdoctoral researchers

Funded postdoctoral positions will be announced on the [News](/news/) page. I am also happy to support applications for independent fellowships in quantum computing, quantum machine learning or computational chemistry. Please get in touch at least two to three months before the funder's deadline, as many schemes need an internal selection round at the University first.

#### Undergraduate and master's students

If you are a student at Liverpool looking for a final-year or MChem project, or a student elsewhere interested in a summer project, I am happy to chat about what might be possible.
